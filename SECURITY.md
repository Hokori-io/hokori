# Security

Please report a vulnerability privately, never in a public issue: write to contact@hokori.io with what you found,
how to reproduce it, and what it affects (the contracts, the SDK, the MCP server or the market data).

We answer within three working days and keep you informed until it is fixed. Please give us a reasonable time to fix
it before you disclose it.

Out of scope: the volatility of any coin's price, and coins' names, pictures or descriptions, which their creators
choose (report those through the site's Report button instead).
